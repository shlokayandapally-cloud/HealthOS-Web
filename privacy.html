HealthOS is a personal health organization tool. It is designed to help users store and organize information they choose to enter. It is not a medical provider and does not provide diagnosis or emergency services.

Information you enter

Depending on the features you use, HealthOS may store profile information, health history, medication and supplement records, vital readings and report files.

Account security

Accounts are authenticated through the configured authentication provider. Database and file access should be protected with per-user access rules.

Your choices
